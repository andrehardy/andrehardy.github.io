As She Is SeenSummary

The Enchantress wears the one face each beholder most desires and speaks in the one voice each listener cannot refuse, and none of it is illusion: the deep has made her flesh able to become what is wanted.

An Enchantress is a Tier III being who reshapes her own body and releases scent and sound tuned to a target's instincts, producing trust and desire strong enough to override the target's judgement.

The Account of the GlanceHow the power works

The Account of the GlanceHow the power works

The Enchantress is never seen as she is. Each beholder sees the face that beholder has always hoped to see, and hears a voice so gentle that refusing it would feel like cruelty. Sailors have turned their ships onto the rocks for her; knights have given up their oaths, and kings their crowns, and none of them felt it as anything but love.

It is not an illusion, and that is what makes her terrible. The deep earth made her flesh changeable, and she changes it. Bone shifts and skin takes on another colour, until the face in the doorway is the face that was wanted. Her breath carries a sweetness that dulls every suspicion, and her song finds the place in the listener where trust is kept, and opens it.

The old tales call her a witch, and the Registry does not. A Witch must gather, dry and boil every working from the ground; the Enchantress needs no pot, because she is herself what the pot would make. Morgan le Fay, who wore a hag’s face or a queen’s as she chose and never stooped to a cauldron, is the great example, and is filed here.

Her power has edges. She must be near: in sight, in hearing, close enough to breathe. Her changes cost her, and a face held too long wears on the body beneath it. Love that she has made must be renewed, and those who wake from it remember.

Two things she cannot move at all. The dead have no heart to quicken, and walk past her as though she were a stone. And the Sealed Light, which sets every body back to the pattern it was first given, would take her borrowed beauty from her in an instant, and more besides.

Being: Tier III, Alpha or Gamma. Source: the Second Tier, through her own body. Access: Biological persuasion.

The Enchantress, or Siren, is a Tier III being whose body the Second Tier has made changeable. She can reshape her own bone, skin and features, and she releases scent and uses vocal sound tuned to a target's instincts. None of this is illusion. The face is real flesh, the scent is a real chemical signal and the song is real sound.

Together these produce trust, attraction and a desire to please that override the target's judgement. The target remains conscious and believes the feelings are their own. While the effect holds, the target will do almost anything she asks, including acts against their own interest.

Fiction usually calls her a witch. She is filed separately because she prepares nothing: what a Witch must extract from plants and minerals, the Enchantress produces in her own body. Morgan le Fay is the best-known case.

Limits: the effects work only at close range and fade without renewal. Changing her form is painful and draining. The dead and anything without a working nervous system are immune. The Sealed Broadcast returns her altered body to its original pattern, undoing her changes and harming her.
