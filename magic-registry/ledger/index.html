  1. MisfiledTelevision

    Evelyn Poole

    Penny Dreadful, John Logan, for Showtime and Sky, 2015

    Called “witch” in the work; filed here as the Warlock.

    Evelyn Poole is called a witch and is filed as a Warlock, because her youth and her power are gifts from her master below, and they leave her the moment his hold on her is broken.

    Evelyn Poole is filed as a Warlock rather than a Witch because her powers are granted by the Devil in return for her service, and are withdrawn when the arrangement fails.

  2. MisfiledBooks and plays

    Harry Potter

    Harry Potter, J. K. Rowling, 1997

    Called “wizard” in the work; filed here as the Sorcerer.

    Harry Potter is the world's most famous wizard and the Registry files him as a Sorcerer, because in his world magic is born, never learned by those born without it.

    Harry Potter is called a wizard but is filed as a Sorcerer because his world's magic is an inherited capacity that study can train but never create.
