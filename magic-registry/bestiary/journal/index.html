The Journal

A field book of what was seen, and when. It stays in the reader’s own house, and nothing written in it leaves that house.Observed states, retained by the reader alone. A line exists only for a condition that was in force at the time of the visit.

The book is open, and no state has yet been witnessed. A line is entered when a room is seen while its condition holds: the full moon, daylight on the stone, the season, the Sabbath, or a fire festival.No observation is on record. A line is written only while a room’s condition is in force.
