Field Reports

Accounts of the sixteen, taken from the old tales, the stages and the screens, and sealed under the name each one truly bears. The misfiled stand first, where a keeper looks before trusting a label.Confirmed portrayals across seven media, each filed by mechanism rather than by label. Rejected labels lead the register.
