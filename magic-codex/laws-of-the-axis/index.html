Directive 2 · Law of the Outward Flare

  1. Geometry of the Flare. Expansion itself generates space and linear time, flaring from the narrow spout (Altitude 100) into the wide cooling bell (Depth 90).
  2. Not a spatial funnel. The flare is a figure of time’s expansion, not a cone seated in space; there is no privileged spatial center.
  3. One-Way Curtain / Veil of Saclas. The Artificer’s instruments are outward-pointing vectors. Like a lantern whose beam lights the stone ahead but cannot shine back into its own oil-chamber, he governs all below him, but looking toward Altitude 100 he meets only opaque primordial mist.
  4. Illusion of the Center / Ahamkara (Maya). Density-bound intelligence perceives itself as immovable center. Not a moral failing — structural law of the office that stamps geometry onto heavy mud. Maya remains the named synonym for this blindness in the comparative apparatus.

Directive 3 · Three Laws of Practitioner Resonance

A practitioner does not create force from nothing; they either negotiate with, tune into, or risk being overwritten by the entities native to their depth.

  1. Luminiferous Buoyancy. Cosmological Tier-1 spark in Tier-4 clay naturally rises. Vault practitioners drop ballast; Mantle practitioners take lithic or contractual ballast to sink.
  2. Non-Local Entanglement. The Spark remains entangled with Time Zero; acts are sympathetic tuning, not projectiles across miles.
  3. Over-Polished Lens / Unintentional Mirror. A throttled sensory instrument polished so finely it reflects the whole cascade — why Folio Fixed and Unhoused entities answer when the operator tilts the mirror to their stratum. The Spark is fractal: the whole cascade is present in the part, which is why a throttled human instrument, polished finely enough, reflects strata far above and below its seat.

Directive 4 · Three Degrees of the Vessel

Degrees describe a vessel’s mode of perception, not an exclusive class ladder. A practitioner may hold a Chair while remaining source-free. The Alchemist’s four stages of the Work are a separate craft sequence and are never renamed as these Degrees.

  1. Submersion (Unawakened Vessel). Pure Artificer intent; Bestiary windows appear empty.
  2. Conceptual Aperture (Scholar’s Sight). Geometry, Furnace, and Orrery detect Tier 2–3 scaffolding; the eye still sees stone and star.
  3. Full Resonance (Twelve Chairs of the Axis). Deliberate breach — vetted grace, Keys of Ingestion, congenital blood-fire, Hidden Note, Counter-Contract, and the rest. Available now through The Trials.

  • Folio F (Fixed): Kernel Location equals Vessel Location. Terminate by rupturing the metabolic loop (bleed Lycanthrope; solar-expose Troll lungs; scrub Golem inscription).

    • U.1 / U.2: close the optical or ingestion aperture, or ground atmospheric charge
    • U.3: cannot neutralize; avert the eye or interpose a Cleric
    • U.4: thermally cool and seal in brass or lead
